The Fortress Comes to Your Desktop

Today we're making Fortized available as a desktop application — and it's been one of the most requested features since day one.

The desktop app isn't just a browser wrapper. It's a dedicated experience built around the way people actually use Fortized — always open, always connected, always ready. System tray integration, native notifications, and startup launch mean the Fortress is there the moment you need it.

One Installer, Every Platform

We didn't want anyone left out. The Fortized Setup works on Windows, macOS, and Linux — one download covers all three. No hunting for the right version. No platform-specific pages. Just download and go.

This is version 1.0.0 — the foundation. We're already working on push-to-talk, custom keybinds, and hardware-accelerated Party Rooms for the desktop client. The Fortress just got a lot closer to home.
