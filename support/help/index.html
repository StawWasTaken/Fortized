Quick answers

The things people ask the most.

What is Fortized?
Fortized is a next-gen social platform where communities come to life. Create or join Bastions (community servers), chat with friends via DMs, customize your presence with the Atelier, and build the community culture you want — all in a safe, beautifully designed environment.
What is a Bastion?
A Bastion is a community server you create or join. It's your fortress with organized chambers (Text, Voice, Forum, Announcement, Poll), member ranks with custom roles, and complete control over who can do what. Owners manage everything; members engage in their spaces.
How do I send a DM?
Click any user's profile and tap "Send Message" — or find them in your Friends list and message directly. Group DMs are available too. All messages are encrypted end-to-end, and you control who can message you with friend request safety checks.
What is Radiance and the Atelier?
The Atelier is where you customize your Fortized experience. Earn Onyx currency through quests and daily rewards to unlock Radiance perks: premium appearances (Midnight Citadel, Obsidian, etc.), exclusive badges, 100MB file uploads, custom emojis, and more. No paid subscription required.
Is my data stored securely?
Absolutely. Fortized uses secure storage with encryption for all data. Your messages and account information are protected. We never sell your data to third parties. We comply with GDPR and DSA. For details, see our Privacy Policy.
How do I stay safe on Fortized?
Fortized has multi-layer safety: mutual-friend checks for DMs, raid protection auto-lockdown for Bastions, advanced AutoMod for message scanning, granular permission controls, and age restriction enforcement. Report any issues via the in-app report system. The Fortized Security Department monitors 24/7.
How do I create a Bastion?
Tap the + button in the sidebar or on your profile, select "Create Bastion", choose a name and icon, and you're in. From there, add chambers (text, voice, forum), set up ranks, and invite members. Your fortress, your rules.
How do I report a user or message?
Hover over any message and click the three dots, or tap the flag icon on a user's profile. Select your reason, add details, and submit. Our moderation team reviews all reports. You can also block users directly from their profile or in DMs.
