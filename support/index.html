quick answers

the things people ask the most.

Is Fortized really free?
Yes. Every feature, every cosmetic, every Bastion. We earn from optional Radiance trades and a creator marketplace cut - never paywalls.
Where is my data stored?
In EU data centers. We're a European company and we keep things local. Some content is cached on a CDN to keep things fast.
Can I import my server from somewhere else?
Yes - we have importers for the big platforms in beta. Open Settings → Import inside any Bastion you own.
How do I report someone?
Long-press (or right-click) the message or profile and choose Report. Our moderation team reviews everything within 24 hours.
Can I delete my account?
Yes - Settings → Account → Delete account. Anything you've posted is purged within 30 days.
