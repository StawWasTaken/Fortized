Builders & Developers

Creator policy.

The rules for everything you build, publish, or integrate as a Fortized creator: ads, bots, bastion templates, marketplace listings, API keys, OAuth and oEmbed integrations, takedowns, and revenue.

Last Updated: 25 April 2026
Effective: 25 April 2026
Creator Terms
Contents
Read this before you create. The Creator Hub gives you tools that reach other people in the realm — your ads sit in front of strangers, your bots run inside other communities, your templates seed entire bastions, and your API keys let third-party software act on your behalf. With those tools come responsibilities. This Policy describes them.
1

Becoming a Creator

1.1 Who Can Create

Anyone with an active Fortized account in good standing can use the Creator Hub. Suspended, banned, or restricted accounts cannot create, publish, or list anything. If your account is moderated mid-creation, all in-progress drafts are paused until the moderation is resolved.

1.2 What You Agree To

Before you can enter the Creator Hub for the first time, you must accept the Terms of Use, the Terms of Service, and this Creator Policy. Accepting once is enough — we won't ask you again unless we publish a material change.

1.3 You Are Responsible

Anything you create — an ad, a bot, a template, an API integration — is published in your name and tied to your account. You are responsible for the content, behavior, security, and consequences of your creations, including any harm they cause to other users.

2

Ads

2.1 What Ads Are For

Ads are paid placements that promote your bastion, your content, or (where allowed) an external destination. They appear in main content areas (banner) and sidebars (rectangle) across Discover, the Forum, and other surfaces.

2.2 Cost and Duration

An ad costs 15 Onyx for 4 days of placement. You can renew (15 Onyx for another 4 days) or enable auto-renew. Editing an active ad costs 5 Onyx. Cancelling an active ad costs 5 Onyx; the ad immediately stops serving and disappears from your inventory 2 hours after cancellation.

2.3 Content Rules

Ads must follow the Terms of Use. They must not contain or promote:

  • Sexual or sexually suggestive content, nudity, or sexualised minors (any depiction)
  • Hate speech, harassment, or content targeting a person or group based on identity
  • Real-world violence, gore, or shock content
  • Drugs, weapons, gambling for real money, or illegal goods and services
  • Scams, phishing, malware, fake giveaways, or deceptive download buttons
  • Misleading impersonation of Fortized, Swiftaw, staff, or other users
  • Off-platform recruitment for groups whose primary purpose violates these rules
2.4 Linking Rules

By default, ads link to a bastion you own. In-app destinations (Home, Discover, Forum, Atelier sub-tabs) are also allowed. External URLs (any domain other than fortized.com) are reserved for Fortized staff and verified partners only. Submitting an external URL without authorisation will get the ad rejected and may cost you the listing fee.

2.5 Takedowns and Refunds

Swiftaw may take down any ad that violates this Policy or the Terms of Use, with or without notice. Onyx spent on a taken-down ad is forfeited. Onyx spent on an ad you cancelled yourself is also forfeited (the cancellation fee is the cost of stopping the placement early). Where a takedown was clearly our error, contact Support for a manual review and possible refund.

3

Bots

3.1 What Bots Can Do

Bots are automated accounts that run inside bastions you authorise. They can react to messages, run commands, post content, manage roles, and respond to events — strictly within the permissions you grant them and the bastion owner accepts.

3.2 Permissions and Scope

A bot has only the permissions selected at creation and granted by each bastion that installs it. Granting a permission you don't need is a security risk. Requesting permissions you don't actually use is grounds for unlisting from the Marketplace.

3.3 Prohibited Bot Behaviour
  • Spam, mass DMs, mass mentions, or unsolicited promotion
  • Scraping or exfiltrating user data beyond what's needed for the bot's stated function
  • Bypassing rate limits, moderation, or other Fortized safeguards
  • Pretending to be a Fortized system bot, staff, or another user
  • Running anything that would violate the Terms of Use if a human did it
  • Cryptocurrency, NFT promotion, or pay-to-play gambling mechanics
3.4 Marketplace Listing

Listing a bot in the Creator Marketplace is optional. Listed bots must have a clear name, description, accurate permission list, and either be free or priced in Onyx. Misleading listings (wrong description, hidden permissions, hidden monetisation) will be removed. Listings can be paid for with Onyx earned through Creator revenue.

3.5 Disabling and Takedowns

Swiftaw may disable any bot that violates this Policy. Bastion owners may remove a bot from their bastion at any time. If a bot is found to be malicious, all installations of that bot may be force-removed across the platform.

4

Bastion Templates

4.1 What a Template Contains

A template is a snapshot of a bastion's structure: channels, categories, roles, default permissions, and certain non-personal settings. Templates do not contain messages, members, member roles, attachments, or any private data from the source bastion.

4.2 Exporting

You can export a template only from a bastion you own. By exporting and listing it on the Marketplace, you grant Swiftaw a non-exclusive licence to host and distribute the template within Fortized so other users can import it.

4.3 Attribution

The original creator's name is shown on the listing. Importers receive a fresh copy of the structure they can modify freely; no ongoing relationship to the original is implied.

4.4 Resale and Pricing

Templates can be free or priced in Onyx. The same listing rules as bots apply: clear name, description, accurate contents. Templates that re-package another creator's work without permission will be removed.

5

API Keys and Integrations

5.1 What an API Key Is

An API key is a credential tied to your account that lets third-party software act on Fortized in a limited way: signing users in with their Fortized account ("Login with Fortized"), rendering a bastion member list, or showing a "Join Bastion" button via oEmbed. A key gives the holder the ability to call Fortized on your behalf — treat it like a password.

5.2 Your Responsibility
  • Keep your keys secret. Never publish them in client-side code, public repos, screenshots, or chat.
  • Rotate (revoke and regenerate) any key you suspect has leaked.
  • Use one key per integration so revoking one does not break the others.
  • You are liable for everything done with your key, including misuse by a service you authorised.
5.3 Allowed Use Cases

API keys are intended for legitimate integrations such as: authenticating Fortized users on your own site, embedding a public bastion's member count or join button, syncing roles with an external service the user opted into, or building a community-side tool for your own bastion. Use cases not listed are not necessarily forbidden, but anything that abuses a user's trust will be revoked.

5.4 Prohibited Use
  • Scraping or storing user data beyond what your integration actively uses
  • Building shadow profiles or selling Fortized data to third parties
  • Sending traffic to bypass rate limits, moderation, or anti-spam controls
  • Using OAuth login to harvest credentials, tokens, or to phish
  • Re-implementing core Fortized features in a way that competes with or extracts value from the platform without authorisation
5.5 OAuth and oEmbed

Login-with-Fortized uses an OAuth-style flow: a user is redirected to Fortized, signs in, and explicitly authorises the scopes your application requests. We will only release the data the user consented to. oEmbed widgets (member list, Join button) render content from a public bastion and may be embedded on third-party sites.

5.6 Rate Limits and Revocation

Keys are subject to per-user and per-key rate limits. Exceeding them will throttle or temporarily disable the key. Swiftaw may revoke a key at any time for abuse, security incidents, or policy violations.

Heads up. The full OAuth, oEmbed, and "Join Bastion" flows are rolling out in stages. The rules in this section apply the moment the corresponding feature is exposed to your account.
6

Revenue and Onyx Earnings

6.1 What You Earn

If you list a paid bot or paid template on the Creator Marketplace, you earn Onyx for every sale, less Swiftaw's platform share. The exact split is shown on the listing form before you publish. Free items earn no Onyx.

6.2 Onyx Is Not Cash

Onyx is a virtual currency tied to your Fortized account, governed by the Fortshop Policy. It cannot be redeemed for real money and cannot be transferred outside the platform.

6.3 Refunds and Chargebacks

If a buyer disputes a Marketplace purchase and we issue a refund, the corresponding Onyx is reversed from your earnings. Repeated disputes against your listings may lead to delisting.

Intellectual Property

7.1 What You Keep

You keep ownership of original content you create — your ad imagery, bot scripts, template structures, and the code behind your integrations. You may use, sell, or remove your work at any time, subject to this Policy and the Terms of Service.

7.2 What You Grant Fortized

By publishing on Fortized you grant Swiftaw a worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free licence to host, store, display, distribute, and route your content as needed to operate the platform. The licence ends when you remove the content, except for backups and audit copies kept for the period required by law.

7.3 You Don't Use What Isn't Yours

Don't upload anything you don't have the right to use. Submitting copyrighted material, trademarks, or another person's likeness without permission may get the work removed and your account moderated.

8

Conduct, Enforcement, and Takedowns

8.1 Moderation

Creator content is reviewed by a mix of automated systems and human moderators. We act on user reports, automated signals, and our own audits. We do not pre-review every piece of content.

8.2 Actions We May Take
  • Hide or delete an ad, bot, template, or listing
  • Disable a bot in all bastions where it is installed
  • Revoke API keys
  • Suspend or close your Creator access while keeping the rest of your account active
  • Suspend or terminate the entire account where the violation is severe or repeated
8.3 Reporting Abuse

If you believe a creator is breaking this Policy, report the offending ad, bot, template, or integration to Fortized Support. Include the creation's name, the creator's username, and a clear description of the issue.

8.4 Appeals

If we take action on something you created, we will tell you what was actioned and why, where we can. You can appeal through Support. Appeal decisions are made by a moderator who was not involved in the original decision wherever possible.

9

Termination and Changes

9.1 You Can Stop Any Time

You can cancel ads, unlist bots and templates, and revoke API keys at any time. Existing licences in the hands of buyers are not retroactively cancelled by you stopping; refer to Section 6 for refunds.

9.2 We May Stop You

Swiftaw may suspend or terminate your access to the Creator Hub for breaches of this Policy or the Terms of Use. Where the breach was minor and unintentional, we will normally warn you first.

9.3 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy. Material changes will be announced in-app or by email at least 30 days before they take effect, and you will be asked to re-accept before continuing to use the Creator Hub. Non-material clarifications take effect immediately and are listed at the top of the page.
