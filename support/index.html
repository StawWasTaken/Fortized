⚙️ Technical
How do I reset my password?+
On the login page, click "Forgot Password" and enter your registered email. Because Fortized currently uses local browser storage, your credentials are stored on your device — clearing browser data will remove your account locally.
How do I delete my Fortized account?+
Navigate to Settings → Account → Delete Account. EU users have the right to full data erasure under GDPR. If you require additional assistance, reach out through our support channels.
Can I change my username or display name?+
Yes. Head to Settings → Profile to update your username, display name, and profile picture at any time. Changes are reflected immediately across all Bastions.
What is the minimum age to use Fortized?+
Fortized requires users to be at least 13 years old globally. In EU member states, the minimum age is 16, or 13 with verifiable parental/guardian consent.
How do I create a Bastion?+
Click the "+" button in the Bastion sidebar to open the creation flow. Set your Bastion's name, visibility (public or private), and configure initial Chambers. As the owner, you have full administrative authority at all times.
What are Chambers and Ranks?+
Chambers are organized channels within a Bastion — text, voice, or announcement. Ranks are the role system with unique permissions and visual identifiers, giving your community clear hierarchy from day one.
Can Fortized staff access or override my Bastion?+
No. Bastion Sovereignty is a core principle. Stawdio Group never overrides or bypasses admin controls. The only exception is enforcement of our Terms of Use in cases of severe violations such as illegal content.
What is Radiance and how do I get it?+
Radiance is Fortized's premium status, unlocked entirely through community engagement — no credit card required. Earn Onyx by completing quests, inviting friends, and claiming daily rewards in The Atelier.
What perks does Radiance include?+
Active Radiance members receive: a custom Radiance Shield badge, an animated avatar border (Profile Glow), extended messaging (up to 4,000 characters), a custom profile banner, and 45MB uploads. Radiance+ adds custom fonts and the ability to gift Radiance to friends. All perks are cosmetic. The Atelier also offers free appearances like Midnight Citadel and purchasable ones like Onyx Pure.
How do I report a user or message?+
Right-click or long-press any message to access the "Report" option. Reports go directly to the Fortized Security Department for review. For urgent incidents, escalate via our support channels below.
What does AutoMod do?+
AutoMod is Fortized's real-time content moderation system. It detects and acts on policy-violating messages before they reach other users. Bastion owners can also configure custom keyword filters.
I've been wrongly banned. How do I appeal?+
Submit an appeal through official support channels including your username and a description. EU users have the right to contest termination decisions under the EU Digital Services Act. We respond within 15 business days.
Why did my account data disappear?+
Fortized currently stores account data in your browser's localStorage. Clearing your browser cache or switching browsers will remove local data. Cloud sync will be introduced in a future update.
Is Fortized available as a desktop or mobile app?+
Fortized is currently accessible via web browser. A dedicated desktop app and mobile application are on the roadmap. For the best experience, we recommend a modern Chromium-based browser on desktop.
I found a bug or security vulnerability. What do I do?+
Report bugs through official support channels or the in-app feedback tool. For security vulnerabilities, contact the Fortized Security Department directly and do not publicly disclose until reviewed. Responsible disclosure is greatly appreciated.
