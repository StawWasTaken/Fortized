Section 01
Overview

Fortized ("we," "our," or "the platform") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information is collected when you use Fortized, how it is stored, and your rights regarding that data.

🔑
Key FactFortized currently operates with local browser storage. Your account data lives on your device, not on remote servers, unless explicitly stated otherwise in a future update.
Section 02
What We Collect
Data collected upon registration and use

When you create a Fortized account or use the platform, the following data may be stored:

  • Your email address, used for account identification
  • Your chosen username and display identity
  • Your password (stored locally; never transmitted in plain text)
  • An optional profile picture you upload
  • In-platform activity such as Bastion membership, friends list, and DMs
  • Usage preferences and platform settings
Section 03
How Your Data Is Stored
Local storage architecture

All account data is currently stored in your browser's localStorage. This means:

  • Data stays on your device and is not synced to external servers
  • Clearing your browser data will erase your Fortized account locally
  • Data is not accessible to Fortized staff or third parties

We approach platform security in layers — from account-level protections to application-side safeguards. Every component is built with your trust in mind.

🏰
Bastion SovereigntyYour Bastion is yours. Our security architecture ensures admins retain full, uncompromised control over their communities at all times. Bastion-related data — including membership records, roles, and DMs — receives the same protections as personal account data.
Future cloud storage

Future versions of Fortized may introduce cloud storage. If and when this happens, this policy will be updated and users will be notified in-app in advance.

Section 04
What We Never Do
Hard commitments
  • We never sell your personal data to third parties
  • We never use your data for targeted advertising
  • We never share your private messages with anyone outside the platform
  • We never access your profile picture beyond rendering it in-app
Section 05
Trust & Transparency

We believe that security only works when it's visible. The Fortized Security Department is committed to publishing regular updates on our practices, incident responses, and ongoing platform improvements. We don't hide our walls — we build them in the open.

🔍

Visible Security Practices

We publish updates on how we handle threats, incidents, and improvements to platform defenses as they happen.

⚡

Incident Disclosure

In the event of a security incident affecting user data, we will notify affected users promptly and transparently.

🛡️

Layered Defense

Security is applied at every layer — from account authentication to community-level access controls.

🏰

Community Sovereignty

Bastion owners hold full administrative authority over their communities. We never override or bypass admin controls.

Section 06
Your Rights
Access, modification, and deletion

You have the right to access, modify, or delete your personal data at any time. Because Fortized stores data locally, you can do this directly through your browser settings or through your account profile page.

🇪🇺
GDPR Rights (EU Users)EU users have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability, and the right to object. If you have concerns about your data or wish to request removal from any future cloud systems, contact us through official Fortized support channels.
Section 07
Changes to This Policy
Update notification

Fortized reserves the right to update this Privacy Policy at any time. Material changes will be communicated via an in-app notice. Continued use of the platform after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

📋
30-Day Notice for Material ChangesIn compliance with EU consumer law, material changes to this policy will be announced at least 30 days before taking effect. We will never silently update terms that affect your rights.
