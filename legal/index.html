02

How Your Data Is Stored

2.1 — Storage & Security

Your account data lives on secure, encrypted servers maintained by Swiftaw. Here's how we handle it:

  • All data is encrypted both while it's being sent to our servers and while it's sitting there
  • We keep redundant backups so your data doesn't just vanish if something goes wrong
  • Your account syncs across devices — log in from your laptop or your desktop, same experience
  • No one on the Fortized team can read your raw data without going through proper authorisation channels
  • Active accounts keep their data for as long as the account exists. Deleted accounts get wiped from our systems within 30 days
Bastion Sovereignty: Your Bastion is yours. Admins keep full control over their communities at all times — we don't interfere with that. Complete audit trails and access logs are maintained.
2.2 — Data Retention

We keep your data as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we remove your data from our live systems within 30 days and from backups within 90 days — unless the law requires us to hold onto something longer.
