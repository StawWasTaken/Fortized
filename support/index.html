Common Questions
Frequently Asked Questions
How do I create an account on Fortized?+
Visit fortized.com/signup and follow the registration steps. You'll need a valid email address and to meet the minimum age requirement (13 globally, 16 in the EU or 13 with parental consent).
What is Radiance and how do I get it?+
Radiance is Fortized's premium status, unlocked entirely through community engagement — no credit card required. Earn Onyx by completing quests, inviting friends, and claiming daily rewards in The Atelier. Once you have enough Onyx, redeem it for Radiance status in the shop.
What is Onyx and how does The Atelier work?+
Onyx is Fortized's earn-only currency. You cannot buy it — you earn it through daily logins, quests, inviting friends, and community events. The Atelier is Fortized's cosmetic shop where you can spend Onyx on appearances, Radiance status, and other items.
How do I create and manage a Bastion?+
Click the + button in the Bastion sidebar to open the creation flow. Set your Bastion's name, visibility (public or private), and configure initial Chambers. As the owner, you have full administrative authority including Ranks, permissions, and moderation tools.
How do I report a user or safety concern?+
Right-click or long-press any message to access the "Report" option. Reports go directly to the Fortized Security Department for review. For urgent incidents, use the contact form below with the "Safety Concern" category.
How do I appeal a ban or suspension?+
Use the contact form below and select "Ban Appeal" as the category. Include your username, the approximate date of the action, and a clear explanation. EU users have the right to contest decisions under the Digital Services Act. Appeals are reviewed within 15 business days.
How do I create bots on Fortized?+
Fortized supports custom bot creation for Bastions. You can create bots through the Developer Portal in your settings. Bots can interact with messaging, events, and moderation features within your Bastion.
Can I change my username or display name?+
Yes. Head to Settings → Profile to update your username, display name, and profile picture at any time. Changes are reflected immediately across all Bastions.
Why did my account data disappear?+
Fortized currently stores account data in your browser's localStorage. Clearing your browser cache or switching browsers will remove local data. Cloud sync will be introduced in a future update.
How do I delete my Fortized account?+
Navigate to Settings → Account → Delete Account. EU users have the right to full data erasure under GDPR. If you require additional assistance, use the contact form below.
