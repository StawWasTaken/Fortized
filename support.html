⚙️ Technical
How do I reset my password? +
On the login page, click "Forgot Password" and enter your registered email. You'll receive reset instructions shortly. Because Fortized currently uses local browser storage, your credentials are stored on your device — clearing browser data will remove your account locally. If you're locked out, contact support for further assistance.
How do I delete my Fortized account? +
You can delete your account at any time via your profile settings. Navigate to Settings → Account → Delete Account. Under GDPR, EU users have the right to full data erasure. If you require additional assistance or want to confirm removal from any future cloud systems, reach out through our support channels.
Can I change my username or display name? +
Yes. Head to Settings → Profile to update your username, display name, and profile picture at any time. Changes are reflected immediately across all Bastions you're a member of.
What is the minimum age to use Fortized? +
Fortized requires users to be at least 13 years old globally. In EU member states, the minimum age is 16, or 13 with verifiable parental/guardian consent, in compliance with GDPR and the EU Digital Services Act. Stawdio Group, registered in France, is subject to applicable EU consumer and digital law.
How do I create a Bastion? +
Click the "+" button in the Bastion sidebar to open the creation flow. You'll be prompted to name your Bastion, set its visibility (public or private), and configure initial Chambers. As the owner, you have full administrative authority over your community at all times.
What are Chambers and Ranks? +
Chambers are organized channels within a Bastion — text, voice, or announcement chambers. Ranks are the role system: each rank can have unique permissions, display names, and visual identifiers. Fortized's military-inspired naming gives your community clear hierarchy and purpose from day one.
Can Fortized staff access or override my Bastion? +
No. Bastion Sovereignty is a core principle of Fortized. Stawdio Group never overrides or bypasses admin controls. Your Bastion's structure, membership records, roles, and DMs belong to you. The only exception is enforcement of our Terms of Use in cases of severe violations, such as illegal content or platform-wide abuse.
What is Radiance and how do I get it? +
Radiance is Fortized's premium status, unlocked entirely through community engagement — no credit card required. Earn Onyx by completing quests, inviting friends, and claiming daily rewards in The Atelier. Exchange your Onyx for Radiance packs ranging from 24-hour boosts to full 30-day memberships.
What perks does Radiance include? +
Active Radiance members receive: a custom Radiance Shield badge, an animated avatar border, extended messaging (up to 4,000 characters), and a custom profile banner. All perks are purely cosmetic and do not affect community moderation or permissions within Bastions.
What happens when my Radiance expires? +
When your Radiance period ends, your account reverts to standard status and premium cosmetics are removed. Your Onyx balance is preserved — you can earn more and re-activate Radiance at any time through The Atelier.
How do I report a user or message? +
Right-click or long-press any message to access the "Report" option. Select the relevant violation category and submit. Reports go directly to the Fortized Security Department for review. For urgent or serious incidents, you can also escalate via our support channels below.
What does AutoMod do? +
AutoMod is Fortized's real-time content moderation system. It detects and acts on policy-violating messages before they reach other users — including spam, slurs, phishing links, and known harmful content patterns. Bastion owners can also configure additional custom keyword filters for their communities.
I've been wrongly banned. How do I appeal? +
If your account was suspended or terminated and you believe it was in error, you can submit an appeal through official support channels. EU users also have the right to contest account termination decisions under the EU Digital Services Act. Include your username, a description of the situation, and any supporting context. We will respond within 15 business days.
Why did my account data disappear? +
Fortized currently stores account data in your browser's localStorage. This means clearing your browser's cache or cookies, using a private/incognito window, or switching browsers will remove your local data. We recommend noting your credentials securely. Cloud sync will be introduced in a future update.
Is Fortized available as a desktop or mobile app? +
Fortized is currently accessible via web browser. A dedicated desktop app and mobile application are on the roadmap. For the best experience during Beta, we recommend using a modern Chromium-based browser on desktop.
I found a bug or security vulnerability. What do I do? +
Please report bugs through our official support channels or via the in-app feedback tool. For security vulnerabilities, contact the Stawdio Safety Department directly and do not publicly disclose the issue until it has been reviewed. Responsible disclosure is greatly appreciated and helps protect the entire community.
